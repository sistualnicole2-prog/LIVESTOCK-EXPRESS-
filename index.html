How Our Logistics Process Works

1. Customer Booking

Customer provides pickup location, destination, livestock type, quantity, date, time, and special requirements.

2. Order Verification

Our logistics coordinator checks shipment details, vehicle requirements, route, and documents.

3. Vehicle Preparation

The assigned vehicle is checked for cleanliness, ventilation, flooring, gates, barriers, and safety equipment.

4. Animal Loading

Animals are loaded carefully by trained handlers to minimize stress, injury, and overcrowding.

5. Transportation

The driver follows the planned route while the logistics team coordinates the delivery.

6. Delivery

Animals are unloaded carefully and delivery details are recorded and confirmed.
